The Queer Liberation March was started in 2025 when members of the Madison Area Democratic Socialists of America, noticing the lack of an existing pride march in Madison, voted to organize their own march for queer liberation with explicitly socialist demands. Organizers from MADSA soon invited other socialist organizations to join in organizing the march, including Trans Resistance Action Committee, Socialist Alternative, Wisconsin Bail Out the People Movement, and the Party for Socialism and Liberation. In June of 2025, this coalition hosted the [first annual Madison Queer Liberation March](https://www.channel3000.com/news/madison-coalition-to-host-inaugural-queer-liberation-march/article_7d7d6cfe-e64c-46e2-a9ac-1d71857112f4.html). Shortly after, we formalized a coordinating committee to organize future marches, as well as fundraising and educational events, centered around the following points of unity:
- Opposition to capitalism and all forms of oppression, including but not limited to homophobia, transphobia, sexism, and racism
- Emphasis on organizing the broad working class and understanding of the importance of democratic, collective decision-making
- Education, healthcare (including gender affirming care and abortion), and housing are human rights
- Solidarity with Palestine and all workers of the world and rejection of Pinkwashing
- Opposition to all legislation that prevents queer and transgender people from full participation in society
- Solidarity with immigrants and rejection of ICE
