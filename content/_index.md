The Queer Liberation March is an annual march for socialism and queer liberation orgianized by a coalition of several socialist organizations in the Madison area including [Madison Area DSA](https://dsamadison.org/), [Trans Resistance Action Committee](https://madisontrac.org/), [Socialist Alternative](https://socialistalternative.org), [PSL](https://pslweb.org/), and [WiBOPM](https://wibailoutpeople.org/).
