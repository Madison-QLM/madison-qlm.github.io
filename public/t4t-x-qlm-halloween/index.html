T4T x QLM Presents: Nightmare on Wilson St

2026-09-21

The QLM organizing committee is hosting a fundraising event for the June 2027 march in collaboration with T4T, a monthly queer/trans dance party. The event will take place on Friday, October 16 at 8pm at the Cardinal Bar. Activities include a raffle featuring pieces from local artists, specialty food and drinks, a costume contest, and dancing with DJ Saint Cat and DJ Rainer! Email contact@madisonqlm.org with any questions.
